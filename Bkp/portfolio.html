About me...


Hello everyone, my name is Juan Manuel Gross, I am years old and I am currently an e-commerce analyst at Inc SA (Carrefour).

I feel passion about technology, especially computer science and programming, due to the wide range of tools it provides us for performing various everyday tasks.
My passion for programming arose from my curiosity about the behind-the-scenes aspects of applications and websites.

In 2013, I started my own business 'CompuGross', focusing on IT technical services and the installation and maintenance of security cameras.
Additionally, I work as a freelance developer, creating and maintaining applications and websites.
